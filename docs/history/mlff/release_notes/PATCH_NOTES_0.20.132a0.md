# mdstats 0.20.132a0 - MLCV-MON1

MLCV-MON1 implements the second gate of the conventional cross-validation correction roadmap. It changes monitor materialization and diagnostic evidence only; MLCV-STOP1 and later checkpoint-selection/export semantics remain for subsequent gates.

## Role-correct target monitors

- CV fold jobs now use `V_i_light`, a deterministic <=256-configuration subset of that fold's DATA5 nested checkpoint-selection domain `V_i_full`.
- Final-development jobs use `D_light`, a deterministic <=256-configuration subset of complete final-validation `D_full`.
- The untouched outer CV fold remains `fold_evaluation.xyz` and is never supplied to the MACE validation loader during fold training.
- Each job also persists `target_checkpoint_full.xyz` for the later run-local full-selection gate.

## TRUE_DFT replay monitors

- The complete independent true-label replay validation corpus is staged as `R_full`.
- A deterministic <=512-configuration `R_light` subset is used for per-epoch lightweight replay monitoring.
- Replay light/full membership and label lineage are immutable in `mdstats.mlcv-monitor-catalog.v1`.

## Training diagnostics

- Every job receives a deterministic <=256-configuration target-training diagnostic subset drawn from its exact gradient-training membership.
- MACE evaluates this subset every validation epoch through the target head and logs it under `target_train_diagnostic`.
- The diagnostic loader is prepended ahead of the ordinary validation loaders, preserving MACE 0.3.16's target-last checkpoint/patience scalar. It has no stopping, ranking, or acceptance authority.
- Per-run `training_history.json`, `training_history.csv`, and `validation_history.png` are reconstructed from persisted MACE metrics with zero reporting-time inference.

## Serialization and compatibility

- DATA8 advances to schema v5 / parser `0.20.132a0` and persists `mlcv_monitor_catalog.json`.
- Historical DATA8 v1-v4 readers remain supported.
- Existing completed campaign identities are not reinterpreted.
- The shipped static `campaign.toml.example` is corrected to the three-seed default already generated by `init`; a regression test now keeps those surfaces synchronized.
- The default campaign remains multi-head replay only with three optimizer seeds and three common CV folds plus one final fit per seed (12 runs).

## Deferred deliberately

This gate does not yet remove the historical lightweight 30/30 candidate eligibility rule, alter the 80%/120% adaptive stop logic, implement per-run top-five ranking/full selection, aggregate CV representatives, or change final production/committee authority. Those changes belong to MLCV-STOP1 and later gates.
