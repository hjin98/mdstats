# mdstats MLFF Target-Training Order Scientific Method

This paper is the sole current D1 owner for the MLFF `TargetTrainingOrder` / `pi_train` membership-design surface: pre-order selector evidence, multi-view training-population coverage, hard membership support, configured-shell repair meaning, complete-order continuation, and independent membership qualification.

It was reconstructed from the mature pre-P6 multi-view selector lineage, independently reviewed under Protocol 6.3, and stakeholder-ratified on 2026-09-15 against reviewed candidate `815823494c88969944eee8f58a6cf107d97bcc09`.

This paper remains the scoped target-order source owner; later editorial changes do not alter the imported Protocol 6.4 authority unless the top-level kernel is explicitly revised.

The target-order method does not obtain authority from historical label-domain/fold fan-out or the historical fixed target-size universe.

## 2. Scientific role of the order

The target-size method uses one deterministic candidate-independent multi-view training order, exact nested training prefixes, one target-size evaluation ladder, common candidate-independent training preparation, paired optimizer seeds, and the accepted short-horizon comparison policy.

The order is constructed from the already-frozen training population before candidate training. Its scientific purpose is to make progressively larger prefixes cover the accepted scientific variation of the training population while preserving mandatory support. Configured prefixes are independently qualified for membership coverage/support before training. Those membership gates are not model-accuracy gates and do not rank already-qualified target sizes.

## 3. Authorized pre-order evidence

The exact `P_train` population is the sole target-order domain. Membership evidence is frozen before any target-size candidate training and may use only evidence belonging to `P_train` or authenticated partition-independent ancestors applicable to those frames.

The restored baseline uses these evidence classes:

1. universal structural/environment evidence, including accepted material-neutral local-structure summaries and raw pair-geometry/coordination evidence;
2. target-development response evidence: accepted labels and derived physical-response channels of frames already assigned to `P_train`;
3. profile selection evidence only when an accepted current material/profile provider explicitly supplies selection-stage features/environment classes;
4. foundation-weakness evidence only when the target-size training protocol itself uses an authenticated frozen foundation checkpoint, using pre-candidate residuals on exact `P_train`;
5. condition, event, extent, and protected-correlation support evidence needed to define hard membership obligations.

Using `P_train` labels to design `P_train` membership is intentional training-design evidence. It does not make those labels held-out validation evidence.

The following may not influence `pi_train`: `M3/M1/M2` labels or predictions, target-size candidate outcomes, reducer evidence, post-selection CV or replay-monitor evidence, calibration/locked/challenge data, or downstream physical/deployment evidence.

Any selector-specific fitted transform, reference distribution, residual, scale, density, local radius, or equivalent quantity is fitted on its declared authorized domain, which for this restored post-split order is exact `P_train`.

`TargetCoverageReference` is the sole selector-specific fitted numerical owner. Historical DATA7 contributes lineage/input semantics needed to authenticate selector inputs but does not create a second fitted metric/scaler/PCA/reference owner.

## 4. One canonical multi-view training order

Let

$$
P_{\mathrm{train}}=\{x_1,\ldots,x_n\}
$$

be the exact training population from the accepted `U_size -> P_train + M3` split. The target-size method constructs exactly one deterministic permutation

$$
\pi_{\mathrm{train}}=(x_{(1)},x_{(2)},\ldots,x_{(n)})
$$

before target-size candidate training. Candidate membership is exactly

$$
T_N=\pi_{\mathrm{train}}[:N].
$$

Thus `N_a < N_b` implies `T_{N_a} \subset T_{N_b}` with exact prefix identity. There is no independent per-`N` membership selector.

### 4.1 Distinct scientific measures

The method keeps four measures distinct:

- configuration cardinality: `N` counts selected configurations;
- coverage reference mass: each represented P1 correlation unit receives equal total mass within a feature family and participating witnesses divide that unit mass;
- training-loss influence: P3 objective/property/configuration weights and masks remain separate and cannot choose membership;
- evaluation estimand: accepted P3 `M1/M2/M3` target-force evaluation/reducer semantics remain separate from membership coverage.

Correlation-balanced coverage therefore does not redefine target size as an effective-sample count.

### 4.2 Required multi-view evidence

The order represents training-population variation through required families covering:

- universal local structure/environment;
- pair geometry and coordination;
- target-development force/energy/thermodynamic/strain/stress response channels defined by accepted training evidence;
- accepted profile-specific selection features when an active provider supplies them;
- frozen foundation-model weakness/residual channels when the target-size method itself is foundation-based.

The exact numerical catalog and applicability rules are D2-owned. A family whose governing provider is not part of the current protocol is absent; an unrelated provider is not acquired merely to populate the selector.

### 4.3 Correlation-balanced reference coverage

For required family `m`, let `W_m` be its reference witnesses and `mu_m` the normalized empirical reference measure assigning equal total mass to each represented P1 correlation unit and equal mass to witnesses within that unit.

Each witness has an authorized local neighborhood in the fitted family metric. For selected set `S`, define

$$
C_m(S)=\mu_m\left(\{w\in W_m: w\text{ has at least one selected representative in its local neighborhood}\}\right).
$$

The hard criterion is

$$
C_m(S)\ge 0.95
$$

for every required family under the D2 numerical tolerance. No active named-family threshold override was instantiated in the coherent recovery chain; introducing one is a new D1/D2 policy change.

For extent-bearing channels, selected support must reach both the lower 1% and upper 99% reference quantiles. These are training-membership support criteria, not accuracy or deployment-adequacy thresholds.

## 5. Canonical hard membership obligations

One canonical obligation authority combines restored automatic obligations with current explicit hard-support policy.

Where applicable, automatic obligations require at least one selected frame from:

- every current P2 condition represented in `P_train`;
- every represented P1 correlation interval/unit;
- every recognized represented structural-event type;
- every represented active profile-environment class;
- both sides of every required extent-bearing channel.

Current explicitly configured hard-support obligations remain policy inputs and may strengthen automatic baselines with larger positive minimum counts.

For a source obligation `o`, define its scientific support locus, exact incidence, and requirement strength separately:

$$
L(o)=(\text{kind},\text{applicability scope},\text{family or none},\text{target selector/identity},\text{relation/side},\text{applicability domain}),
$$

$$
A(o)=\text{exact candidate-incidence set on current }P_{\mathrm{train}},
$$

$$
k(o)=\text{positive required minimum count}.
$$

Requirement strength and source-local identifiers are not part of locus identity.

If accepted automatic and explicit requirements describe the same support locus, have the same accepted applicability/provider semantics, and project to the same exact incidence set, they form one canonical obligation with

$$
k_{\mathrm{canonical}}(L)=\max_{o\in L} k(o).
$$

For example, automatic `condition=A, minimum=1` plus explicit `condition_id=A, minimum=2` means one canonical requirement `count(A) >= 2`, not a contradiction and not two hard-support votes.

Source aliases remain provenance only. Equality of incidence alone does not establish semantic aliasing. Different scientific loci remain distinct even if their incidence is identical. A purported same-locus disagreement in incidence, applicability, provider identity, family/side/target meaning, or an ambiguous projection into current `P_train` fails closed.

P2 `condition_id` remains the sole target-size condition identity. P1 correlation-unit identity remains a separate protected relation/provenance concept used for weighting, mandatory support, and representation balance; it may not recreate historical label-domain target-size partitions.

If complete `P_train` cannot satisfy the required family/obligation policy, preparation fails as method infeasibility. Requirements are not weakened to manufacture a target size.

## 6. Coverage-progressive order

While a canonical hard obligation or required-family coverage criterion remains unsatisfied, candidate priority is, in order:

1. reduce hard-obligation deficits;
2. advance the least-covered required family;
3. advance total required-family coverage;
4. balance representation across P1 correlation units;
5. improve representative coverage utility;
6. improve sparse diversity.

After hard requirements are satisfied, the same order continues by representative utility, correlation balance, and sparse diversity. Exact numerical ranking, ties, tolerances, and equivalent optimized execution are D2-owned.

This behavior is candidate-independent: it depends only on frozen pre-candidate evidence and the selected prefix accumulated by the deterministic order itself.

## 7. Configured-shell repair and complete continuation

At configured target-size shell boundaries, deterministic repair may alter only the newly added active shell; every completed smaller configured prefix is immutable. A replacement is admissible only when it preserves canonical hard obligations, does not regress required-family coverage beyond D2 tolerance, and strictly improves the accepted repair objective.

Repair modifies the one master order; it is not an independent per-size selector. After the largest configured shell, the same multi-view method continues deterministically from the final repaired prefix until every `P_train` frame is ordered. There is no UID-only suffix and no unconfigured repair shell.

## 8. Independent membership qualification

Every configured candidate prefix is independently qualified from authenticated primitive selector evidence. A candidate is eligible for the target-size learning experiment only when:

- the exact prefix exists;
- required training labels are usable;
- every required multi-view family passes hard mass coverage and required extents;
- every canonical hard obligation passes at its effective minimum.

Because configured prefixes are nested and the predicates are positive support/coverage predicates, qualification is monotone under unchanged authority: `FAIL* -> PASS*`. `PASS -> FAIL` is an invariant violation.

Coverage qualification may reject inadmissible membership, but among qualified candidates it has no ranking or tie-break authority. The accepted P3 target-force reducer and practical-equivalence rule remain the sole target-size comparison authority.

- canonical hard-obligation authority and effective minima;

- independent MVQUAL evidence for every configured prefix;

Execution-only queue/backend/timing state is not scientific provenance unless required to interpret a qualification environment.

## 10. Falsification and reopen conditions

The D1/D2 method is violated if any of the following occurs:

- `pi_train` is generated by condition-round-robin/UID fallback rather than the accepted multi-view method;
- a required family or support obligation is omitted without accepted applicability reason;
- selector fitting uses `M3`, held-out CV, calibration/locked evidence, candidate outcomes, or downstream evidence;
- foundation residual evidence uses an unauthenticated foundation identity or frames outside exact `P_train`;
- a scratch/non-foundation protocol acquires a foundation provider solely for `pi_train`;
- current P2 condition identity is replaced by historical label-domain/fold target-size topology;
- same-locus aliases are counted as multiple hard-gain votes;
- a stronger valid same-locus explicit minimum is rejected instead of composing by `max(k)`;
- conflicting same-locus incidence/applicability is silently merged;
- an unqualified configured prefix is admitted to target-size training;
- qualification changes PASS to FAIL at a larger nested prefix under unchanged authority;
- repair changes an earlier configured prefix or regresses required-family coverage beyond D2 tolerance;
- ranks after the largest configured candidate are completed by UID/arbitrary fallback;
- execution width/backend/chunking/restart changes the scientific order, repair trace, or qualification result.

D3/D4 may not compensate for such violations by lowering thresholds, adding rescue sizes, fabricating provider evidence, or creating a second target-order owner.

## 11. D1 -> D2 handoff

D2 must preserve:

1. one exact `P_train` domain and one complete deterministic `pi_train`;
2. separate configuration-count, correlation-balanced coverage, training-loss, and evaluation measures;
3. accepted required multi-view roles and applicability;
4. uniform `0.95` hard family coverage and lower-1%/upper-99% extent-support meaning;
5. one canonical automatic + explicit hard-obligation set with locus identity independent of strength, same-locus identical-incidence requirements composed by strongest minimum, and semantic/incidence conflicts failing closed;
6. current P2 condition identity and separate P1 correlation-unit identity;
7. exact candidate-independent MVSEL2 ordering and deterministic tie behavior through complete-order continuation;
8. active-shell-only repair preserving lower configured prefixes and non-regressing support;
9. independent MVQUAL qualification, monotonicity, and separation from target-size model-error ranking;
10. the leakage boundary excluding `M3`, candidate outcomes, and downstream evidence from membership construction.

D2 may choose only numerically equivalent execution methods inside these semantics. D3/D4 optimization, persistence, resource, and concurrency choices remain downstream.
