## 1. Purpose

This ledger is the lossless field/input disposition required by R1. This ledger is evidence for the proposed R1 candidate only. It does not promote accepted authority.

## 11. Family threshold census and disposition

### 11.1 Historical normative hook

The integrated chain specification states default hard family coverage `0.95` unless an explicitly identified material/profile policy defines another threshold for a named family. Therefore the reconstruction cannot simply erase the possibility without checking whether such a policy was instantiated.

### 11.2 Coherent-snapshot evidence

At `3937881...`:

1. `TargetCoveragePolicy` owns exactly one scalar `coverage_threshold`, default `0.95`, with no family-threshold map.
2. MVSEL2 validation/selection accepts one scalar coverage threshold.
3. MVQUAL2 owns one scalar `coverage_threshold`, default `0.95`.
4. selector qualification evidence records `coverage_threshold: 0.95`.
5. no recovered selector policy record, material/profile coverage policy map, or qualification identity found in the coherent target-order chain supplies a different named-family threshold.

### 11.3 Adjudication

The named-family override sentence is therefore retained as evidence of a **dormant/uninstantiated extension point**, not an active recovered method capability. The proposed restored baseline maps every applicable required family to `0.95` under one TargetCoveragePolicy identity. Any future per-family override is a new D1/D2 policy revision, not a D4 configuration freedom.
