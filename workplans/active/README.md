# Active workplans

Active workplans are temporary engineering coordination and do not define current mdstats behavior by themselves.

There is one active MLFF implementation workplan:

- `MLFF_TARGET_SIZE_TRAINING_PRIORITY_EVALUATION_LADDER_ARCH_RESET_WORKPLAN.md` — consolidated architecture reset for the single target-size study: training-priority correlation-safe role pools, one rich target-training order, one frozen nested M1/M2/M3 evaluation ladder, configurable power-of-two target/evaluation cardinalities, exact n1/n2/n3 continuation, `Nmax + M3` nominal target-size capacity, post-selection group-safe validation-only CV, fresh final production, destructive retirement of fixed/complement/domain-prefix semantics, and preservation of accepted shared training/resource ownership.

All previously active plans and target-size amendments were retired on 2026-08-28 to `../archive/retired-2026-08-28/` after their still-valid obligations were reconciled into the consolidated plan. Archived plans are historical records only and do not independently impose active gates.

Full long real-data/GPU production qualification remains separate from routine functional acceptance and is deferred to the established final release/GPU qualification phase. Scientific M-ladder sampling qualification must be reported separately and may be `deferred/unavailable` when representative evidence is not available; it must not be reported as passed without evidence.
