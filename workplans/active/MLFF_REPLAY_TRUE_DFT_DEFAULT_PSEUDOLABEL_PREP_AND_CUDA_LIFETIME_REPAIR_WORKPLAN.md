# MLFF replay TRUE_DFT default, pseudo-label preparation, and CUDA lifetime repair

## Background and terminology

This workplan is a Protocol 6.2 Software Design D3 -> D4 handoff for one coupled defect family: the configured replay-label policy is not aligned with the newly required default, expensive foundation pseudo-label preparation can occur inside `cross-validate` before TRAIN2 scheduling, and the cold pseudo-label cache builder can leave an internally created CUDA MACE provider resident after its final replay consumer.

**TRUE_DFT replay** means replay training labels come from the authenticated source density-functional-theory (DFT) labels contained in the selected replay source. **Foundation pseudo-label replay** means replay training labels are generated by the bound foundation MACE model for the replay geometries. These are distinct scientific label authorities and must remain explicit in method identity.

A **single-source replay campaign** uses `[paths].replay_set` as the one external replay corpus. Deterministic train/monitor membership, true-label views, pseudo-label views, prediction caches, and other replay transport artifacts are reconstructable internal materializations derived from that source and the resolved replay policy.

**Prepare-owned replay materialization** means expensive or durable replay derivation needed by later training is completed and authenticated by the `prepare` operation. `cross-validate` may consume and validate that prepared state but must not silently become the owner of a replay-wide foundation-inference pass.

**CUDA residency** means accelerator memory still held by live framework/provider state or the process allocator after a CUDA operation. A completed prediction call is not a completed resource lifetime. The owner that acquires a MACE/CUDA provider must retire it at its final consumer boundary on success and failure.

## 1. Outcome and authority

### 1.1 Protected stakeholder/product outcome

For new single-source replay campaigns:

```text
[paths].replay_set absent
    -> no single-source replay

[paths].replay_set present
    + [replay].label_mode omitted
        -> TRUE_DFT replay                         [default]

    + label_mode = "true_dft"
        -> TRUE_DFT replay                         [explicit equivalent]

    + label_mode = "foundation_pseudolabel"
        -> foundation pseudo-label replay          [explicit opt-in]
```

The default path must be the simplest path: it authenticates the replay source, derives the deterministic split and required true-label transport, and performs no replay-foundation model inference. If required source DFT labels are missing or invalid, the TRUE_DFT path fails closed with an actionable label-domain error. It must never silently fall back to pseudo-label generation.

Foundation pseudo-label replay remains fully supported as an explicit configuration choice. Its prediction/qualification/materialization workload is preparation work. A later post-selection cross-validation invocation must start from authenticated prepared replay state rather than unexpectedly launching a replay-wide foundation prediction pass.

### 1.2 Governing upstream constraint and domain boundary

The choice of replay training labels changes scientific training-data provenance and therefore is not invented by D3. The stakeholder decision governing this cycle is:

- source TRUE_DFT labels are the default replay-training authority for newly resolved single-source configurations;
- foundation pseudo labels are an explicit optional replay-training authority;
- choosing either mode remains part of the recorded training/replay scientific identity;
- historical explicit choices retain their meaning.

D3 owns how configuration resolves that already-given policy, how replay preparation is staged, where durable artifacts live, and where accelerator/resource ownership begins and ends. D4 implements those decisions. If an accepted D1/D2 document currently states that pseudo labels are the mandatory/default scientific method rather than an optional mode, that current-owner text must be reconciled to the stakeholder decision before durable closure; D3 must not silently rewrite scientific rationale to manufacture agreement.

### 1.3 Accepted D3 architecture being concretized

Preserve the current architecture in which:

- one external `replay_set` is the source authority and internal train/monitor/pseudo/true transports are reconstructable descendants;
- replay label mode is part of replay/method identity and changing it invalidates only materially dependent descendants;
- true-label and pseudo-label namespaces are distinct;
- the deterministic replay split remains one authority shared by both label modes;
- foundation-model providers and other large accelerator owners are released when their final preparation consumer completes;
- a replay cache stores content evidence, not a live provider;
- `cross-validate` owns post-selection CV, not replay-wide source preparation;
- the existing TRAIN2 scheduler evaluates the actual aggregate VRAM baseline presented to it and may correctly resolve zero safe admission.

The existing P5 TRAIN2 zero-safe-admission work remains binding. This plan removes a pre-TRAIN2 source of contaminated baseline; it does not weaken or bypass the scheduler.

### 1.4 Applicable D1/D2 and external/domain constraints

Do not alter, except for the explicitly authorized default label-policy change above:

- replay source geometry membership;
- deterministic train/monitor split ratio, seed, or split algorithm;
- target/replay head semantics;
- target membership or target-size selection;
- post-selection fold membership, purge/exclusion, seeds, or horizons;
- optimizer family, optimizer-state semantics, learning-rate schedule, EMA, AMSGrad, weight decay, clipping, or training batch size;
- global loss coefficients or target/replay head weighting;
- model precision/dtype policy;
- configured acceleration backend or CuEq/e3nn training semantics;
- checkpoint selection, replay-monitor interpretation, target-only CV acceptance, or fresh final-production semantics;
- pseudo-label prediction numerical identity, foundation checkpoint/head identity, qualification thresholds, or label values merely to reduce memory.

Changing replay label mode changes method identity. Existing evidence created under pseudo-label training cannot become TRUE_DFT evidence by reinterpretation, and vice versa.

### 1.5 Stable D4 contracts/specification

The existing `ReplayLabelMode.TRUE_DFT` and `ReplayLabelMode.FOUNDATION_PSEUDOLABEL` values remain the canonical normalized representations. `ReplaySingleSourceConfig.content_digest` continues to bind the effective label mode. Deprecated/legacy forms may be accepted only through central normalization to one of those canonical values.

An omitted `label_mode` in a new single-source configuration resolves to `TRUE_DFT`. An explicit historical compatibility `mode` retains its recorded meaning rather than being ignored and reinterpreted under the new default.

### 1.6 Explicit non-goals

Do not:

- change the TRAIN2 90% VRAM envelope, per-job estimate, zero-safe admission, live-memory safety, or CPU/GPU scheduler policy to make contaminated replay residency fit;
- add `torch.cuda.empty_cache()` at `cross-validate`/scheduler entry as the primary fix for an unretired owner;
- introduce a second replay cache family, provider registry, GPU lease manager, scheduler, monitor daemon, retry database, or persistent resource state solely for this repair;
- make pseudo-label mode the fallback when source true labels are absent;
- change pseudo-label prediction batch semantics, precision, backend, stress/force calculation, or scientific thresholds merely to reduce the observed 19.9 GiB high-water mark;
- remove pseudo-label support;
- alter target-size, CV, final-production, or checkpoint science;
- rewrite historical frozen records in place.

## 2. Cycle decisions and delegated D4 space

### 2.1 Frozen for this cycle

1. **TRUE_DFT is the omitted-value default for single-source replay.** The canonical resolver owns this default exactly once.
2. **Pseudo-label training is explicit opt-in.** Foundation inference is authorized only when the effective single-source replay mode is `foundation_pseudolabel`.
3. **Missing TRUE_DFT labels fail closed.** There is no automatic scientific fallback to pseudo labels.
4. **Doctor remains validation/preflight, not the expensive prediction owner.** It may validate configuration, source accessibility/schema, model capability needed by an explicit pseudo mode, and already-present prepared state, but it must not perform the full replay-wide pseudo-label inference pass.
5. **Prepare owns replay derivation needed downstream.** TRUE_DFT prepare is CPU/I/O label/split/materialization work. Explicit pseudo mode may use the accelerator during prepare, but its accelerator lifetime ends inside prepare.
6. **Cross-validation consumes prepared replay state.** It authenticates current replay authority/materializations and fails actionably if required prepared state is absent/stale/corrupt. It does not silently repair that condition by running replay-wide foundation inference.
7. **Pseudo-label provider ownership is explicit.** A provider constructed inside the cold prediction-cache builder is owned there and is always closed after its final replay prediction consumer, including exceptions. A caller-supplied provider remains caller-owned unless its interface explicitly transfers ownership.
8. **One static inference executor spans the cold replay prediction operation.** The executor is bound to the actual configured prediction device and preserves bounded OOM batch learning across outer replay batches instead of recreating that state for each batch.
9. **Allocator cleanup is a consequence, not the ownership contract.** Releasing the owned provider/model/graph references through the existing lifecycle primitive is mandatory. Framework cache release may occur inside that established primitive, but a scheduler-entry cache flush cannot substitute for provider retirement.
10. **Expensive cache decisions are observable.** Prepare must make it clear whether pseudo-label prediction is a cache hit, a cold build, or a rebuild because stored state is unusable. Exact wording/mechanism is delegated; silent replay-wide GPU work is not acceptable.
11. **Historical explicit configuration remains compatible.** Explicit `label_mode`, explicit supported legacy replay `mode`, and persisted normalized method/replay identities continue to mean what they meant when recorded. The new omitted-value default does not rewrite frozen evidence.
12. **Existing TRAIN2 scheduler behavior is preserved.** After this repair it should simply observe a clean/admissible baseline when no unrelated process occupies the device; it remains responsible for refusing genuine external/owned baseline pressure.

### 2.2 Delegated D4 concretization

Implementation may choose local helper factoring, internal return/status types, exact logging text, cache-status representation, and cleanup ordering provided the frozen decisions above hold.

The preferred concretization reuses existing machinery:

- `single_source_replay_config_from_campaign()` as the canonical replay normalization owner;
- existing replay source/split/true-label cache and view builders;
- existing foundation-prediction cache identity and materialization;
- `StaticMaceInferenceExecutor` as the bounded static inference owner;
- `MaceCalculatorProvider.close()` / existing accelerator-residency cleanup as the provider retirement path;
- current campaign store records/currentness checks rather than a new replay readiness database.

The exact function names may be altered by implementation if a simpler equivalent ownership boundary results. They are not durable architecture authority.

### 2.3 Simplification target

This cycle must remove or narrow three accidental behaviors rather than compensate around them:

- remove the requirement that a single-source replay campaign explicitly spell `label_mode` merely to obtain the intended TRUE_DFT default;
- remove lazy replay-wide pseudo-label generation from the post-selection CV path and reconnect it to `prepare`;
- remove per-outer-batch construction of a device-misdeclared static executor and the unclosed internally created prediction provider.

Do not add a later cleanup wrapper around `cross-validate` while preserving the earlier ownership leak. Do not retain both prepare-owned and CV-owned cold-build paths as competing authorities.

## 3. Material implementation obligations

### R1 - canonical replay default and compatibility normalization

#### Concern and required end state

`single_source_replay_config_from_campaign()` currently treats omitted `label_mode` as an error unless a historical `mode` happens to identify a replay label authority. New single-source configuration generation/examples also explicitly steer users toward `foundation_pseudolabel`.

Change the canonical resolution so:

- no `replay_set` -> no single-source config;
- `replay_set` + omitted label selector -> `ReplayLabelMode.TRUE_DFT`;
- explicit `label_mode = true_dft` -> TRUE_DFT;
- explicit `label_mode = foundation_pseudolabel` -> pseudo;
- an explicitly supported historical `mode` continues to normalize to its historical true/pseudo value;
- contradictory simultaneous selectors fail before expensive work instead of choosing whichever caller reads first;
- downstream code consumes the normalized `ReplaySingleSourceConfig`, not its own default.

Whether the compatibility rule gives explicit `label_mode` precedence over a matching old `mode` or rejects duplicate declarations is delegated only if they agree; conflicting declarations must not silently select one scientific method.

#### Acceptance evidence

Focused configuration tests must establish omitted/default, both explicit modes, legacy normalization, conflicting-selector rejection, relative-path resolution, and stable canonical serialization/digest behavior. Existing explicit pseudo-label generated/frozen campaign fixtures must continue resolving as pseudo.

A test that calls a downstream method with a preconstructed `ReplaySingleSourceConfig` cannot close this obligation; the real canonical campaign resolver must execute.

### R2 - TRUE_DFT default path must remain foundation-inference-free

#### Concern and required end state

The default scientific mode should not load the foundation model merely to reconstruct replay labels that already exist in the source.

For a valid TRUE_DFT single-source replay corpus:

- source authentication and deterministic split execute;
- train and monitor true-label descendants are produced/authenticated as required by current consumers;
- replay training policy records `TRUE_DFT`;
- no pseudo-label prediction cache is required for training;
- no replay foundation provider or static foundation inference executor is constructed;
- `prepare`, `cross-validate`, and final-production routing do not reach `_construct_prediction_provider()` on behalf of replay pseudo-label generation.

If true labels are absent/incompatible, fail at the true-label/source owner. Do not infer that pseudo mode was intended.

#### Acceptance evidence

Use a real single-source campaign configuration with omitted `label_mode`, the production replay resolver/materializer, and a replay source containing authenticated true labels. A bounded spy/failpoint may sit at the foundation-provider construction boundary and must remain untriggered. Also prove that explicit pseudo mode triggers the pseudo path, so a trivially disconnected provider helper cannot make the negative test pass.

### R3 - move explicit pseudo-label cold preparation out of cross-validation

#### Concern

Current control flow can call `_single_source_replay_context()` from post-selection replay resolution before the first TRAIN2 scheduler telemetry sample. On a missing/invalid pseudo prediction cache, that context can perform a full replay-wide foundation inference pass. The apparent "pre-recovery-preflight" TRAIN2 baseline is therefore not a command-wide pre-CUDA baseline.

The command contract already tells users that foundation pseudo-label generation is deferred to `prepare`; the actual prepare runtime currently does not own that materialization.

#### Required end state

- `prepare` resolves the effective replay mode and constructs/authenticates all replay state required by downstream training.
- In TRUE_DFT mode this is source/split/true-label work only.
- In explicit pseudo mode, `prepare` owns prediction-cache build/reuse, pseudo qualification, deterministic split/materialized pseudo training view, and any required TRUE_DFT monitor/view under the existing scientific contract.
- Prepared replay authority is persisted/authenticated through existing campaign records/currentness mechanisms.
- `cross-validate` reads/authenticates the required prepared replay state and never invokes replay-wide foundation inference.
- Missing/stale/corrupt required prepared state produces an actionable preparation/currentness failure. It does not cause `cross-validate` to become a hidden repair stage.
- Re-running `prepare` on a valid cache/state is idempotent/reuse-oriented and does not recompute foundation predictions.

Do not create a second durable "replay prepared" authority if existing replay source/cache/split/view records can express the required currentness. Consolidate around the smallest existing record set that lets consumers authenticate the prepared state.

#### Acceptance evidence

Real command-owner tests must cover `doctor -> prepare -> cross-validate` routing for TRUE_DFT and explicit pseudo modes. The CV test must execute production post-selection replay resolution; seeding the already-resolved in-memory context or mocking the CV owner is not sufficient.

For explicit pseudo mode include cache-hit and cold-cache preparation. The cold-cache case must prove the foundation prediction owner fires during prepare and cannot fire during the following cross-validation invocation.

### R4 - close the cold pseudo-label CUDA owner at its real boundary

#### Concern

On a cold prediction-cache build, `build_replay_foundation_prediction_cache()` constructs a `MaceCalculatorProvider` when no provider is supplied. The current function returns the completed cache without explicitly closing that internally created provider. PyTorch can consequently retain the CUDA allocation high-water mark in the parent process after predictions stop, producing approximately 19.9 GiB at 0% utilization before TRAIN2 admission.

#### Required end state

- Track whether the prediction provider was constructed by the cache builder or supplied by its caller.
- Internally constructed providers are retired in exception-safe cleanup after the final prediction/cache-build consumer.
- Caller-supplied providers remain caller-owned unless ownership was explicitly transferred by an already-defined interface.
- Cleanup occurs on success, provider validation failure after construction, source iteration failure, prediction failure/OOM terminal failure, shard/audit write failure, publication failure, and any other path leaving the builder.
- The existing provider `close()`/accelerator-residency cleanup primitive is reused; do not invent a second CUDA lifetime subsystem.
- The completed cache contains only durable scalar/array/content evidence and no live provider/executor/model reference.

#### Acceptance evidence

Focused lifecycle tests require an ownership-sensitive fake provider whose `close()` can be observed, plus failure injection at multiple post-acquisition points. A caller-owned provider test must establish it is not closed accidentally.

A fake-only lifecycle test does not prove CUDA allocator release. Target-host evidence is required in R7 for the real MACE/CUDA lifetime claim.

### R5 - use one correctly device-bound static executor for a cold pseudo-label build

#### Concern

The current `_provider_predictions()` creates a new `StaticMaceInferenceExecutor` for each outer replay batch and does not pass the actual prediction device. The executor therefore defaults to CPU-facing device semantics even while the provider can be CUDA-backed, and its learned safe batch/OOM state is discarded between outer batches.

#### Required end state

- One static inference executor spans one cold prediction-cache build rather than one executor per outer replay batch.
- Its device is the effective `ReplayFoundationPredictionPolicy.device` (or an equivalent canonical provider device binding), so CUDA synchronization/cache/OOM handling follows the real device.
- Bounded OOM learning persists across the replay corpus. If the first large batch learns a smaller safe batch, subsequent outer replay batches do not blindly reset and retry the known-unsafe size.
- Provider ownership passed into the executor is consistent with R4: internally constructed provider may be executor-owned; caller-supplied provider is not silently adopted.
- Geometry order, prediction identity, shard grouping semantics, and scientific prediction values remain unchanged by executor lifetime consolidation.

The exact internal API may be altered if the same behavior is achieved more simply, but recreating executor/OOM state per outer batch is not acceptable.

#### Acceptance evidence

Use a deterministic provider that triggers an OOM-like backoff on the first large batch and records subsequent requested batch sizes. Prove learned safe size persists across later outer batches. Add a device-binding assertion and ordering/content-equivalence checks against the current non-OOM reference behavior.

### R6 - cache-state observability without a new cache authority

#### Concern

The existing cache loader can collapse absent, incompatible, corrupt, or failed-validation cache state into a generic miss. For a replay corpus of production size that miss can trigger a long/high-VRAM foundation prediction pass with little explanation.

#### Required end state

At the user-visible prepare boundary, expose enough information to distinguish at minimum:

- authenticated prediction-cache hit;
- cold prediction-cache build because no current cache exists;
- rebuild because stored cache state is stale/incompatible/corrupt/unusable.

The diagnostic must identify the owning replay stage and effective label mode. It need not expose internal stack traces or create a durable cache-status database. Exact invalidation reason granularity is delegated as long as a costly rebuild is not indistinguishable from a normal hit.

Cross-validation should report that it is consuming authenticated prepared replay state, not imply that it is preparing it.

### R7 - real target-host boundary proof for the 19.9 GiB defect

#### Concern

Prior replay batching/qualification tests used deterministic non-MACE providers and could not prove lifetime of an internally constructed CUDA provider. Previous P5 telemetry began only after replay resolution, so it could not locate CUDA use occurring earlier in the command.

#### Required target-host evidence

On the supported CUDA target host, run at least the following after focused/affected regression passes:

**Case A - default TRUE_DFT, omitted `label_mode`:**

1. start from a clean device baseline;
2. run `prepare` against a representative single-source replay corpus with valid DFT labels;
3. establish that replay preparation performs no foundation pseudo-label MACE inference and leaves no model-scale CUDA residency;
4. run `cross-validate` far enough to reach its initial TRAIN2 admission telemetry;
5. establish that the pre-TRAIN2 baseline contains no replay-foundation model-scale residue and that the scheduler admits/refuses work only according to real current occupancy.

**Case B - explicit pseudo mode, cold prediction cache:**

1. start from a clean device baseline and intentionally remove only reconstructable pseudo prediction/materialization cache state needed to force the cold path; do not delete unrelated valid scientific/restart evidence;
2. run `prepare` and observe the foundation prediction workload;
3. capture aggregate NVML/device memory and, when available, process-local `torch.cuda.memory_allocated`, `memory_reserved`, `max_memory_allocated`, and `max_memory_reserved` at useful acquisition/peak/pre-close/post-close boundaries;
4. establish that provider/executor retirement returns the mdstats parent to ordinary context-scale occupancy after prepare completes rather than retaining the prediction high-water mark;
5. immediately run `cross-validate` and establish that it performs no new replay-foundation prediction pass before TRAIN2 admission;
6. confirm no orphan accelerator process/provider remains after normal completion or controlled interruption.

The evidence need not require the pseudo prediction peak itself to be small. The governed claim is that the expensive owner is bounded, staged correctly, and retired before TRAIN2. If the real pseudo prediction workload intrinsically exceeds the supported prepare-time resource envelope even with correct ownership/backoff, route that separately; do not conceal it by weakening lifetime evidence.

Production qualification for this resource claim is **required before this workplan can PASS**. It may occur at the final implementation qualification gate rather than during every local edit, but absent target-host realization remains explicitly open rather than being called passed by fake-provider tests.

## 6. Authority, documentation, and history impact

### 6.1 D4 specification/configuration

Yes. The omitted single-source `label_mode` behavior changes from invalid/legacy-dependent resolution to TRUE_DFT. Configuration examples/init generation must make pseudo mode visibly opt-in rather than emitting it as the normal generated choice. Documentation must state that explicit pseudo mode changes training method identity.

### 6.2 D3 Architecture Manual

Yes, if the current manual does not already state these decisions precisely. Reconcile at least:

- replay preparation ownership under `prepare`;
- `cross-validate` as a prepared-state consumer rather than a cold replay-preparation owner;
- provider retirement at the prepare owner boundary;
- TRUE_DFT default / explicit pseudo-mode configuration consequence at the architecture-facing configuration boundary.

Do not hand-edit generated assembled/PDF derivatives when chapter-source generation is the repository owner. Regenerate and verify them through the documented publication path. If the required toolchain is unavailable, record the derivative as unavailable/blocking; source edits alone do not make a tracked generated artifact current.

### 6.3 D1/D2 impact

The stakeholder has explicitly changed the default scientific replay label policy. Preserve both existing modes and their scientific identities; no algorithmic change within either mode is authorized. Reconcile any current scientific/method prose that still calls pseudo-label replay the default. If implementing TRUE_DFT default exposes a contradiction in the accepted training method (for example, true-label replay cannot be combined with the accepted multi-head objective without changing label-domain/E0 semantics), stop and route that issue to the scientific/numerical owner rather than patching it in D3/D4.

### 6.4 Semantic evolution/history

A concise semantic-evolution entry is warranted because this change reverses/clarifies a historically important default and because the CUDA defect is a recurring provider-lifetime/stage-ownership class. Record why:

- new single-source replay defaults to source truth;
- pseudo-label replay remains opt-in rather than removed;
- expensive pseudo preparation belongs to prepare;
- provider lifetime must end at the final preparation consumer;
- the 19.9 GiB pre-CV observation was caused by pre-scheduler replay preparation/lifetime, not by permission to weaken TRAIN2 admission.

History must not become a second current default authority.

## 8. Reopen / Challenge triggers

### D4-local blockers

Remain in this workplan when defects are ordinary implementation nonconformance under the frozen decisions, including additional lazy CV call sites, forgotten provider failure paths, stale tests, missing docs, or cache/currentness wiring errors.

### Evidence requiring D3 reopen

Reopen D3 if evidence shows that replay preparation cannot be owned by `prepare` without introducing a second competing authority/state, or that downstream consumers require a live foundation provider beyond the preparation boundary for a material accepted reason.

### Evidence requiring D2/D1 challenge/reopen

Route upstream if TRUE_DFT-as-default cannot preserve the accepted multi-head training semantics without changing the objective/label-domain formulation, if true versus pseudo replay requires a new statistical role not represented by current method identity, or if pseudo prediction numerical settings themselves must change for correctness rather than resource realization.

### Structural complexity requiring simplification before another additive repair

If implementation begins adding a second replay readiness record, CV fallback builder, scheduler cleanup hook, provider registry, or mode-specific parallel call graph to reconcile duplicated ownership, stop and simplify around the existing canonical resolver/prepare/cache/provider owners before adding durable machinery.

### Serious Challenge trigger

Raise `SERIOUS CHALLENGE` only if accepted current authority becomes materially contradictory or unrealizable under the stakeholder policy—for example, one current normative owner requires pseudo labels as the mandatory training truth while another equally authoritative accepted owner requires TRUE_DFT for the same default campaign and neither is superseded. No such contradiction is established at workplan creation.

## 9. Final handoff and PASS criteria

Implementation is review-ready only when the final candidate is snapshot-complete and all of the following are true:

1. omitted single-source `label_mode` resolves canonically to TRUE_DFT;
2. explicit pseudo mode remains supported and explicit historical modes retain meaning;
3. TRUE_DFT default path performs no replay foundation inference and has no pseudo fallback;
4. `prepare` owns explicit pseudo prediction/materialization and `cross-validate` cannot cold-build it;
5. internally owned pseudo prediction providers/executors are retired on every terminal path;
6. one correctly device-bound executor retains bounded OOM learning across the cold prediction build;
7. cache hit/cold/rebuild behavior is observable at the owning prepare boundary;
8. replay/method/cache identities invalidate/reuse only according to semantically relevant effective mode and source/policy identity;
9. existing TRAIN2 zero-safe/live-memory protections remain unchanged and pass affected regression;
10. current documentation/config examples no longer present foundation pseudo labels as the implicit/default single-source training authority;
11. required generated documentation/package derivatives are regenerated and verified or remain explicitly unavailable/blocking;
12. semantic-evolution history records the default/stage/lifetime correction without becoming current authority;
13. target-host Case A proves the default TRUE_DFT path reaches TRAIN2 without replay-foundation model-scale residue;
14. target-host Case B proves a cold explicit pseudo preparation may peak in VRAM but retires its owned CUDA residency before `cross-validate`, which then performs no replay-wide re-inference;
15. final affected regression/integration and dependent P5 evidence applicability are reconciled on the assembled candidate;
16. every required unexecuted check or unresolved material impact remains explicitly blocking rather than being inferred green.

A later independent Software Design Review must reconstruct the final routing/resource/configuration behavior and attempt falsification at the real prepare/CV/provider boundaries. Literal workplan compliance is not sufficient if the candidate still permits hidden pseudo inference, competing replay owners, scientific-mode reinterpretation, or model-scale CUDA residency across the preparation boundary.
