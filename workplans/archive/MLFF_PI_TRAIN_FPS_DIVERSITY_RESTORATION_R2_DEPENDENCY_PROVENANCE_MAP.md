## 2. Classification rule

Every recovered surface is classified as one of:

- **CORE-RECOVER** — accepted P5A6 scientific/numerical executable semantics are represented by the recovered 393 source and independently reconciled in R1; restore only the smallest current-compatible dependency closure and prove oracle equality;
- **LATER-OPT** — later accepted same-semantics execution optimization carried by 393; retain its capability/performance contract while allowing D3/D4 rewiring beneath current owners;
- **DIAGNOSTIC/RESTART** — useful evidence or reconstructible continuation support; it has no independent ranking/scientific authority;
- **REJECTED/RETIRED** — superseded topology or machinery that must not be restored.

`CORE-RECOVER` is a provenance classification, not permission for blind byte copy. Current owner/domain/schema bindings must be re-established explicitly.

## 4. Final OOC / mapped-file capability closure

The final recovery carrier demonstrates the mature resource design that Revision 8 requires D3/D4 to preserve or equal:

1. **NEIGHBOR1 streaming/OOC** — candidate edges stream to disk rather than accumulating one giant anonymous array; final offsets/candidates may be file-backed; finalization uses bounded copy scratch; required output bytes and safety headroom are checked before named finalization; failed finalization removes attempt-owned outputs.
2. **MVIDX OOC inverse** — large candidate-oriented inverse arrays may be built file-backed with bounded chunk scratch and resource-queue admission rather than edge-count-sized anonymous temporaries.
3. **packed/shared restore capability** — final current restoration must preserve the Revision-8 requirement that mapped file descriptors scale O(1) with family count, including forward-only restore mapping only candidate-oriented roots required by MVSEL2/REPAIR2.
4. **legacy cache rejection** — a reconstructible historical per-family persistence layout that would require descriptor-proportional mapping/large sidecar traversal is not migrated. It is rejected as incompatible reconstructible cache and rebuilt from authenticated NEIGHBOR1 authority.
5. **disk/scratch admission and publication** — final durable bytes, temporary/finalization scratch, available/quota-constrained storage when observable, descriptor/inode constraints where material, and concurrent writer/adoption ownership must be admitted before/during OOC build. ENOSPC/write failure may not publish partial accepted state.

These are execution/resource contracts, not changes to D2 ranking.
