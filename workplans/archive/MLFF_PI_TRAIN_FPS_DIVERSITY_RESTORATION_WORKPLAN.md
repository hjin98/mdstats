# MLFF `pi_train` feature-space diversity restoration workplan

## 0. Disposition: SERIOUS CHALLENGE to current target-order adequacy

The current target-size architecture correctly separates **target cardinality selection** from downstream cross-validation and production, and correctly requires every target candidate to be an exact prefix

```text
T_N = pi_train[:N].
```

However, the accepted target-order method is too weak for the scientific experiment it supports. Current D1 says ordering evidence *may* favor representative or diverse configurations; current D2 explicitly permits no priority evidence and then orders each condition bucket by immutable frame UID before condition round-robin. Production preparation currently supplies no training-priority evidence. Therefore the ordinary current path can construct `pi_train` without any structural-space diversity or coverage criterion.

This is a material methodological defect, not merely a D4 wiring omission. The automatic target-size screen attempts to compare models as a function of training-set cardinality `N`; that comparison is interpretable only if the nested sequence `T_N` is generated by one scientifically defensible, candidate-independent membership progression. A deterministic UID ordering can satisfy nestedness while placing redundant configurations early and structurally distinct configurations late. In that case the learning curve confounds cardinality with an arbitrary serialization/order realization.

The required repair is deliberately narrow: **restore a lightweight feature-space coverage/diversity mechanism as the owner of `pi_train` progression without reviving the retired FEAS/MVIDX/MVSEL/REPAIR/MVQUAL topology.** The present split, one-order architecture, exact-prefix invariant, prepared-generation boundary, target-size reducer, post-selection CV, and fresh production architecture remain intact unless concrete falsification forces an upstream reopen.

Historical evidence already establishes that mdstats had exact deterministic maximin/farthest-point sampling (FPS), bounded nearest-distance state, coverage scoring, and production-scale selection implementations. The restoration should reuse or simplify those proven kernels where semantically applicable rather than invent a parallel selector subsystem.

## Background and terminology

**Farthest-point sampling (FPS)**, also called greedy maximin selection, constructs a space-filling sequence. For feature vectors `z_i` and selected set `S_k`, the next point is

```text
i* = argmax_{i notin S_k} min_{j in S_k} d(z_i, z_j).
```

Define the nearest-selected distance

```text
d_i(S) = min_{j in S} d(z_i, z_j)
```

and the covering radius over an authorized population `P`

```text
R_max(S; P) = max_{i in P} d_i(S).
```

Coverage quantiles such as `Q50`, `Q90`, and `Q95` of `{d_i(S)}` characterize how densely the selected set represents the authorized feature population. They are **sampling-support diagnostics**, not claims of force-field accuracy.

A **coverage-progressive order** is one whose successive prefixes intentionally expand representation of the authorized structural feature space instead of relying on arbitrary stable identifiers.

A **condition bucket** is the current categorical target-size grouping already used by P2 (composition/temperature/strain/regime and accepted condition evidence). Condition support and feature-space diversity are complementary: the first prevents one condition from monopolizing early prefixes; the second prevents near-duplicate configurations inside a condition from monopolizing them.

## 3. D1 repair: require a scientifically meaningful nested training sequence

D1 must be amended narrowly around `pi_train`.

### 3.1 Required scientific invariant

Replace permissive wording equivalent to “ordering evidence may favor diverse frames” with the stronger invariant:

> `pi_train` is a candidate-independent, coverage-progressive ordering of `P_train`. Subject to accepted condition/support constraints, successive prefixes must deliberately expand representation of the authorized structural configuration space. Stable identifiers may break genuine numerical ties but may not supply the scientific priority order.

### 3.2 Meaning of target-size comparisons

D1 must state explicitly that target-size selection asks about model behavior as **cardinality increases along one frozen data-selection policy**. Therefore:

```text
change N  -> add the next configurations under the same coverage policy
not
change N  -> expose an arbitrary UID prefix
```

Coverage progression controls membership; EVAL2 still controls empirical model-performance evidence. A good covering radius does not imply a good model, and a poor EVAL2 score cannot be repaired by declaring the geometric coverage sufficient.

### 3.3 Selection measure versus training-loss influence

D1 must explicitly distinguish:

- **selection/support measure:** the geometric/structural population that `pi_train` is intended to represent progressively; and
- **training-loss influence:** weights/masks/objective coefficients applied after membership has been chosen.

For this minimal restoration, selection is over the empirical authorized `P_train` configuration population with explicit condition support. P3 configuration/property weights do not redefine geometric membership. If future evidence shows that weighted optimization influence must alter membership, that is a separate D1 change.

### 3.4 Leakage boundary

Baseline membership evidence must be candidate-independent and available before model training. The minimal baseline is geometry/condition-derived evidence only. DFT energies, forces, stresses, model residuals, checkpoint behavior, CV, EVAL2 outcomes, or production outcomes cannot influence `pi_train`.

### 3.5 Condition support

Retain the existing scientific role of condition support. Diversity must be restored **inside** the condition-balanced progression rather than replacing condition balance with unconstrained global FPS that could starve a scientifically declared condition.

D1 may permit a future globally coupled coverage optimizer only after evidence demonstrates that it preserves condition support at least as well as the baseline. This workplan does not require that redesign.

### 3.6 D1 falsification cases

Before promotion, demonstrate that current UID ordering fails at least one admissible fixture where:

- many near-duplicate configurations have early UIDs while structurally distinct configurations have later UIDs;
- all frames share one condition, so condition balancing alone cannot help; and
- the coverage-progressive order materially reduces the covering radius/upper distance quantiles for the same `N`.

Also test the opposite failure mode: unconstrained global FPS must not be accepted as the baseline if it can omit an accepted condition from an early prefix that the current condition-support rule would represent.

## 4. D2 repair: minimal exact coverage-progressive `pi_train`

D2 must define a reconstructible reference method. The baseline below is frozen for this cycle unless independent D1/D2 review finds a genuine blocker.

### 4.1 Authorized structural feature vector

Construct one candidate-independent **geometry-only** feature vector `z(x)` for each `x in P_train` from current preparation-owned evidence.

Baseline feature families:

1. cell/shape/deformation coordinates already available from neutral/raw structural evidence;
2. material-neutral pair/local-structure coordinates already available from current structural feature providers; and
3. explicit missing indicators only where the accepted geometry provider permits a structurally meaningful missing value.

Explicitly forbidden baseline coordinates:

- total/per-atom DFT energy;
- force magnitudes, force quantiles, species force errors, or any other force-label summary;
- pressure/stress values derived from DFT labels;
- foundation-model residual/difficulty coordinates;
- candidate model predictions or candidate training/evaluation outcomes.

If the current neutral DATA4 representation does not contain enough material-neutral local-structure information to distinguish configurations that are materially different for MLFF support, D2 must route to the existing current structural-selection/DATA6 provider rather than inventing an ad hoc target-size-only descriptor family. The final accepted feature schema and canonical coordinate order must be explicit and content-identified.

### 4.2 Robust normalization

Fit normalization exactly once on the authorized `P_train` feature population before ordering. The proposed reference is coordinate-wise robust centering/scaling:

```text
center_j = type-7 median(z_j)
scale_j  = Q75_j - Q25_j
```

Coordinates with non-finite authorized values are rejected unless their feature owner explicitly defines missing semantics. Coordinates with `scale_j` below the accepted degeneracy threshold are removed from distance accumulation and recorded as inactive; a zero-variance coordinate must not manufacture distance.

D2 must define the quantile convention, binary64 arithmetic/reduction order, active-coordinate ordering, and finite-precision tie semantics explicitly before promotion. Do not copy an unexplained historical `1e-12` tolerance merely because old code used it.

### 4.3 Condition-local representative seed

For each nonempty condition bucket `C`, compute the coordinate-wise median vector in the normalized active feature space and choose the **observed frame nearest that median vector** as the first FPS seed for that condition. Use the D2 canonical identity only as the final exact tie breaker.

This seed is a representative observed configuration, not a synthetic centroid and not a pairwise-distance medoid unless those happen to coincide.

### 4.4 Condition scheduler

Preserve the current condition-support progression as closely as possible. The minimal baseline is the current deterministic condition round-robin over sorted condition IDs. The change in this cycle is **within-condition order**, not a new population scheduler.

For each visit to a nonempty condition bucket:

- if that condition has selected no frame yet, emit its representative seed;
- otherwise emit the remaining frame maximizing its nearest-selected distance to frames already selected from that condition.

This yields one exact order and prevents the restoration from changing both condition allocation and structural diversity simultaneously.

If independent evidence shows that strict round-robin badly distorts established condition mass for large prefixes, route that as a separate D1/D2 scheduling question; do not silently replace the scheduler during this repair.

### 4.5 FPS/maximin rule

For condition `C` with selected subset `S_C`, define

```text
d_C(x) = min_{s in S_C} ||z(x) - z(s)||_2
```

and choose

```text
x* = argmax_{x in C \ S_C} d_C(x).
```

Maintain exact nearest-selected squared-distance state incrementally. A production optimization may chunk/vectorize updates or reuse the mature bounded maximin kernels, but it must be exactly equivalent to an independently simple scalar reference for governed membership decisions.

No dense all-pairs `O(N^2)` persistent matrix is required or permitted merely to restore FPS. The preferred implementation keeps `O(|P_train|)` nearest-distance state plus bounded feature/chunk workspace.

### 4.6 Scope of the exact progression

`pi_train` remains one exact permutation of all `P_train` frames. The implementation may optimize only the prefix needed for currently reachable target sizes **only if** the remaining suffix is still produced by the same defined coverage progression when/if consumed; it may not silently append UID order to satisfy a serialization shape.

If full permutation construction is too expensive at representative scale, D2/D3 must solve that through an exactly equivalent lazy/persistable progression state or another exact algorithm—not by weakening membership semantics.

### 4.7 Coverage diagnostics

For each configured candidate size `N <= |P_train|`, independently score the prefix `T_N` against the authorized full `P_train` feature population using the same accepted metric and record at minimum:

```text
R_max(N) = max_i min_{j in T_N} d(z_i,z_j)
Q50(N), Q90(N), Q95(N)
minimum/median selected-to-selected nearest-neighbor distance
per-condition selected count
per-condition R_max / Q95 where condition cardinality permits
```

Required metamorphic property for exact nested prefixes:

```text
R_max(N_b) <= R_max(N_a) for N_b > N_a
```

up to the explicitly accepted finite-precision comparison semantics.

These diagnostics are evidence and observability. They do not gate target-size success unless a future D1/D2 change explicitly promotes a coverage threshold.

### 4.8 Determinism and identity

The target-order identity must bind at least:

- exact `P_train` membership/split identity;
- exact feature-provider/schema identity and canonical coordinate order;
- normalization policy and fitted normalization identity;
- condition-key policy;
- representative-seed rule;
- FPS distance/precision/tie semantics;
- condition scheduler semantics; and
- resulting `pi_train` digest.

Frame UID may remain a final deterministic tie key but cannot be the priority source when non-tied structural evidence exists.


There is **one** target-order owner. Do not create `CoverageSelector`, `FPSPlan`, `MVSEL3`, or another durable public selector authority alongside `TargetTrainingOrder` unless evidence proves the current owner cannot represent the required contract.

### 5.2 Prepared-generation boundary

All fitted normalization/selection evidence required to reconstruct `pi_train` belongs to `prepare` and the immutable prepared generation. Downstream operations must not reread source files, restore DATA4/DATA6 merely to recompute the order, or refit the metric.

A changed feature schema, metric fit, scheduler, FPS semantics, or `pi_train` creates a new target-size generation and invalidates materially dependent target-order descendants. Unrelated source facts/caches that remain owner-valid are preserved.

### 5.3 Reuse rather than resurrection

Prefer direct reuse/consolidation of the mature kernels in `selection.py`:

- robust feature scaling where semantically identical;
- bounded incremental nearest-distance updates;
- exact deterministic maximin progression; and
- independent coverage scoring helpers.

Do not route current production through the historical `TrainingSelectionPlan`, quota categories, DATA7 ladder owner, or retired multi-view selector records. Extract/rehome only the minimal reusable numerical kernels if ownership would otherwise remain misleading.

## 7. Evidence and oracle requirements

### 7.1 Independent reference oracle

Create a deliberately simple scalar/reference implementation for bounded fixtures that does **not** import the production FPS/order implementation. It must independently compute:

- normalization;
- representative seed;
- condition round-robin schedule;
- condition-local nearest-selected distances;
- FPS choices; and
- coverage metrics.

Production and reference must agree exactly under the accepted finite-precision/tie contract.

### 7.2 Required counterexamples

At least these fixtures must exist:

1. **UID redundancy trap:** early UIDs are clustered; later UIDs span the feature domain. FPS must materially improve fixed-N coverage.
2. **single-condition trap:** condition balancing is powerless; FPS must still produce diverse prefixes.
3. **multi-condition starvation trap:** naive global FPS omits a condition; accepted condition-support/FPS method must not.
4. **duplicate/near-duplicate trap:** duplicate structural points cannot consume the early prefix while distant support remains.
5. **constant-coordinate trap:** a zero-information coordinate cannot alter order.
6. **serialization-order trap:** reordered feature columns reconstructed by canonical semantic keys yield identical metric/order.
7. **UID-renaming trap:** renaming non-tied frame UIDs cannot change membership/order; only genuine numerical ties may resolve differently according to the accepted canonical tie identity.
8. **forbidden-label trap:** perturbing DFT energy/force/stress values while geometry is unchanged cannot change `pi_train`.
9. **restart/currentness trap:** persisted metric/order reload exactly; stale metric or feature identity is rejected.

### 7.3 Comparison against current behavior

On at least one representative real or realistic current dataset, compare the current UID/condition order against the restored method at the same configured `N` values using only coverage diagnostics. The restored method should not be promoted on aesthetics alone: it must demonstrate materially better structural coverage or expose why the chosen feature metric is inadequate.

No target-force accuracy improvement is required to prove that FPS restores the **sampling capability**; model-performance effects remain a separate downstream observation.
